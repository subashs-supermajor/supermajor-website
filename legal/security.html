02

CAD Geometry & Model Isolation

We enforce multi-tiered cryptographic and topological controls over raw manufacturing geometry (STEP, IGES, SolidWorks, CATIA, Parasolid, OBJ):

Encrypted Ingestion Channels & Isolated Repositories

All CAD uploads and asset transfers are enforced via TLS 1.3 in transit. Storage repositories and backup snapshots are encrypted at rest using AES-256 with isolated, customer-partitioned key management (AWS KMS/GCP KMS).

Incoming assemblies are partitioned into dedicated, segregated network shares. No shared asset repositories exist across competing brand engagements. An artist working on Automotive Client A cannot view, query, or mount storage shares allocated to Client B.

Client-Side 3D WebGL & Interactive Asset Protection

Ephemeral 3D Web Previews: Real-time browser viewers utilize lossy geometry optimization, Draco compression, and vertex quantization. Raw solid CAD topology (B-Rep) is never served to client-facing web browsers or accessible via inspect tools.

Internal mechanical tolerances, interior screw bosses, proprietary PCB layouts, and hidden manufacturing marks are algorithmically stripped and decimated before real-time compile. Only the non-sensitive outer aesthetic shell is compiled for web delivery, ensuring reverse engineering is impossible.

03

Strict Zero AI Ingestion Policy

In an era of generative machine learning, industrial intellectual property faces severe exposure risks from cloud AI processors that scrape or retain user inputs for neural training.

Mandatory Studio AI Prohibition & Vendor Commitments

SuperMajor strictly bans the uploading, pasting, batching, or transmission of any client CAD data, technical drawings, CMF documents, unreleased product renders, or proprietary textures to public generative AI tools (including Midjourney, OpenAI/Sora/ChatGPT, Runway, Stable Diffusion, or browser-based AI mesh converters).

All external compute vendors, cloud rendering farms, and API integrations are legally bound by enterprise zero-data-retention and no-training agreements.

All 3D modeling, tessellation, shader craftsmanship, animation physics, and compositing at SuperMajor are produced directly inside enterprise workstation toolchains (such as Blender, SideFX Houdini, Maxon Cinema 4D, and Adobe Creative Cloud) running locally on studio hardware.

04

Physical & Studio Infrastructure

Our studio infrastructure located in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India integrates enterprise-grade physical and network security:

  • Physical Access Security: SuperMajor studio premises enforce electronic biometric/keycard access control. Visitors are escorted at all times; unreleased hardware prototypes are kept in locked, access-logged storage vaults.
  • Clean Desk & Screen Policies: Workstations displaying pre-launch assets are configured to lock automatically after 3 minutes of inactivity. Monitors are positioned away from external glass windows.
  • Hardware Disk Encryption: All production workstations, laptops, and local render farm nodes enforce full-disk AES-256 encryption (BitLocker / FileVault) preventing offline data extraction if a physical drive is compromised.
  • VLAN Network Segmentation: Workstation subnets are segregated from guest networks. Direct peer-to-peer file sharing and external unvetted USB media are disabled at the operating system policy level.
Remote & Distributed Pipeline Governance

Remote pipeline access is strictly governed via Zero-Trust Network Access (ZTNA) with multi-factor authentication (MFA) and encrypted virtual desktop infrastructure (VDI), enforcing disabled local drive redirection, clipboard restrictions, and disabled USB mass storage.

This guarantees that proprietary CAD meshes never sit on unmanaged remote endpoints or personal drives.

05

Identity & Access Governance

We apply the Principle of Least Privilege (PoLP) across all systems:

MFA Enforcement

Hardware-backed Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is strictly required for all internal studio accounts, cloud vaults, and code repositories.

Role-Based Access

Artists only hold access rights to project folders actively required for their immediate production tasks. Administrative privileges are tightly quarantined.

Rapid Deprovisioning

When an artist or contractor concludes their project milestone, access credentials and tokens are cryptographically revoked within 4 hours.

Personnel Vetting & Bilateral NDAs

Human factors are central to confidentiality. SuperMajor enforces rigorous personnel verification:

  • Pre-Employment Background Checks: Every employee, senior technical director, and specialist artist undergoes identity verification, past employment checks, and professional reference vetting.
  • Compulsory Binding NDAs: All team members sign legally binding Non-Disclosure Agreements with SuperMajor that survive termination of employment. Breaches incur immediate legal termination and statutory civil liability.
  • Security Awareness Training: Personnel complete mandatory training on phishing defense, CAD handling protocols, clean screen practices, and prompt incident reporting.

08

Incident Response Protocol

SuperMajor maintains an Incident Response Plan designed to contain, remediate, and report potential security events:

1. Containment: Immediate network isolation of affected workstations or storage buckets within 60 minutes of detection.

2. Forensic Analysis: Determining root cause, affected assets, and scope of exposure using immutable server audit logs.

3. Client Notification SLA (24 Hours):

Affected clients will be formally notified within 24 hours of a confirmed breach or unauthorized asset access incident, accompanied by a preliminary forensic summary and containment action plan.

4. Remediation: Implementing post-incident hardening measures, rotating all credentials, and submitting a comprehensive incident closure report.

Intellectual Property & Licensing

We operate under an unequivocal, transparent intellectual property framework:

A. Pre-Existing Client Intellectual Property

The Client retains sole and absolute ownership of all pre-existing intellectual property, product designs, CAD geometry, engineering tolerances, patents, trademarks, and trade secrets provided to SuperMajor. Commissioning our pipeline transfers zero ownership of your product to SuperMajor.

B. Final Render Deliverables & Licensing

Upon receipt of full and final payment under the applicable SOW, SuperMajor grants to the Client an irrevocable, exclusive, worldwide, perpetual license (or full copyright assignment where expressly stipulated in the SOW) to use, reproduce, broadcast, display, and publish the final delivered render images, animations, video masters, and interactive WebGL assets across all media.

C. Studio Working Files & Internal Pipeline IP

Final transfer of rights applies solely to completed deliverables specified in the SOW. Proprietary working project files (including raw .blend/.max scene graphs, procedural shader setups, and internal pipeline scripts) remain the exclusive intellectual property of SuperMajor unless explicitly purchased under a separate written agreement.

SuperMajor retains full ownership of its underlying procedural animation setups, lighting rig presets, material node graphs, camera rigs, and pipeline automation tools. Delivery of native working scene files requires an explicit written buyout agreement with agreed studio licensing fees.

05

Review Phases & Approval Gates

To guarantee precision and maintain schedule fidelity, our studio pipeline enforces structured stage-gate approvals:

GATE 1:
CAD Tessellation & Pre-Vis: Approval of model accuracy, camera angles, framing, and storyboard timing.
GATE 2:
Clay & Motion Animatic: Approval of camera paths, assembly motion, cut pacing, and mechanical accuracy.
GATE 3:
LookDev & Materials: Approval of shaders, texture fidelity, lighting moods, and color grading.
GATE 4:
Final Render & Master Output: High-resolution multi-pass render output, final compositing, sound design, and master delivery.
Deemed Approval Policy

Milestone Deliverables will be deemed accepted if the Client does not submit written feedback or request specific revisions within 5 business days of delivery.

This mechanism prevents render farm idling and resource gridlock during active production sprints.

Each stage includes standard minor revisions as outlined in the SOW. Changes requested to previously approved stages (e.g., modifying camera motion after look development rendering is initiated) constitute an out-of-scope modification and will be billed at standard studio hourly rates with an updated schedule.

06

Portfolio & Publicity Rights

SuperMajor is a showcase-driven creative studio. We reserve the professional right to display completed visual deliverables in our studio portfolio, showreels, website case studies, and creative awards.

Publicity Embargo Window

SuperMajor will not publicly showcase client visuals or case studies until the client has publicly launched the underlying product or campaign, or after 90 days following final deliverable handover, whichever occurs first.

This safeguard ensures that unannounced industrial prototypes, patent applications, and confidential hardware reveals remain fully protected prior to formal commercial launch.

Strict NDA Precedence: Where a project is executed under a specific Non-Disclosure Agreement or non-publicity rider (such as unannounced defense, industrial, or confidential automotive prototypes), SuperMajor strictly respects the NDA and will never exhibit the work publicly without express written clearance.

Fees, Payments & Invoicing

Standard commercial payment terms:

  • Milestone Payments: Unless otherwise stated in the SOW, standard project billing consists of an advance deposit (typically 40%–50%) upon project initiation, intermediate progress milestones (where applicable), and final balance upon master approval.
  • Currency & Taxes: Invoices are billed in INR (for Indian clients) or USD/EUR/GBP (for international clients). Indian Goods & Services Tax (GST) is levied at the statutory rate of 18% where applicable.
  • Payment Window: Standard payment term is Net 15 days from invoice issuance date. High-resolution master assets and unwatermarked deliverables are released upon settlement of the corresponding milestone invoice.
  • Late Interest: Uncontested invoices overdue beyond 30 days are subject to late interest of 1.5% per month or the maximum permissible by Indian law.
Work Suspension for Overdue Invoices

In the event an invoice remains unpaid past its due date, SuperMajor reserves the right to suspend active rendering pipelines, pause milestone deliverables, and withhold project assets until all outstanding amounts are resolved.

Dedicated compute clusters and workstation allocations will be reallocated during suspensions, and project timelines will extend accordingly.

QUESTIONS

FAQ

What are generative AI services?

They use AI tools to help create images, video and other content from written direction. At Super Major that means creative production led by artists, not software development.

How can businesses use generative AI?

To explore campaign ideas, create backgrounds and scenes, test visual directions and adapt content for different channels.

Can generative AI create product images?

It helps with creative imagery, but it can change shapes, logos and details. Every output is checked, and 3D is the better route where accuracy is essential.

Can AI generate marketing videos?

AI helps with short clips and motion concepts. Finished films usually also need direction, editing and often 3D product animation.

What is the difference between AI-generated visuals and 3D CGI?

AI creates images from learned patterns, which suits ideas and atmosphere. 3D CGI is built from a precise model, so shape and materials stay exact.

Can AI-generated content maintain brand consistency?

Not on its own. Consistency comes from your brand guidelines, art direction and human review.

Can AI visuals be used in advertising campaigns?

Often, after review. Accuracy, intellectual property and advertising rules vary by market, so we cannot give a blanket legal guarantee.

Can generative AI work with existing product images?

Yes, for approved material. We do not upload client CAD, drawings or unreleased renders to public AI tools. See our security policy.

Does AI-assisted content require human editing?

Yes. Our team reviews and refines everything for accuracy, quality and brand fit before delivery.

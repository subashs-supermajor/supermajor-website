How it works

From brief to
final visuals.

A structured workflow that keeps every project on time, on brief and free of revisions that waste budget.

  1. 01

    Brief & references

    We review every input you have — CAD files, technical drawings, reference photos, brand guidelines and usage context. The more we understand about the product and where the visuals will run, the fewer iterations the render needs.

    You provide

    • CAD / drawings / photos
    • Brand & colour specs
    • Output requirements

  2. 02

    3D modelling

    Our team builds an accurate digital 3D model of your product — geometry verified against the CAD, proportions checked, and every surface prepared for materials. This master model is reused across all outputs, so adding variants or angles later costs a fraction of the original.

    We deliver

    • Verified 3D mesh
    • Approval preview
    • Reusable master asset

  3. 03

    Lighting & CGI rendering

    We apply materials, textures and finishes, then set up studio or lifestyle lighting and camera positions matched to your brief. Each scene is rendered at the resolution your platform needs — e-commerce packshots, full-page marketing images or social-ready crops.

    We deliver

    • Photorealistic renders
    • Multiple angles
    • Revision round included

  4. 04

    Final delivery

    Approved files are packaged in the formats your team needs — high-resolution JPG and PNG for print and digital, plus the source 3D files so you own the asset. The master model stays on file for fast turnaround on future campaigns.

    We deliver

    • Production-ready files
    • Source 3D assets
    • Archive for future use

Questions

FAQ

What is 3D product visualization?

It means building an accurate digital 3D model of a product and using it to create realistic images, often before the physical product exists or without a photo shoot.

Do you create 3D models from CAD files?

Yes. CAD is the most accurate starting point. We can also model from technical drawings, reference photos or an existing product.

Can you create photorealistic product renders?

Yes. We set up materials, lighting and camera to match the look your brand needs, from clean studio shots to styled scenes.

Can you visualize different product colours and materials?

Yes. Once the model is built, colours, finishes and materials can be changed on the same asset, so each variant doesn't start from scratch.

Do you provide exploded product views?

Yes, as still images or animation. Our work for Mitsuba India includes exploded views of motor components.

What file formats do you deliver?

Images are delivered in the formats and resolutions agreed for your channels, such as print, web or marketplace listings. We confirm formats in the project plan.

How long does a 3D visualization project take?

It depends on the product's complexity, the inputs you have and the number of images. You get dates in a written plan after we review your brief.

Can the 3D models be used for AR and interactive product viewers?

The same model can be prepared for interactive 3D and AR. Real-time use needs an optimized version, so tell us early if you plan to use it this way.

Can you create visuals for an entire product catalogue?

Yes. For a range, we agree one lighting and camera setup first, so every product in the catalogue looks consistent.

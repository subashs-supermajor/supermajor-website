Questions

FAQ

Can you work from CAD files?

Yes. Send CAD, drawings or reference photos, whatever you have. We reply in writing with what we can build from it.

Is CGI better than product photography?

Not always. Photography is simpler for a handful of products in one colourway. 3D wins when colourways, angles and channels multiply, or when the product doesn't exist yet. One model covers all of them.

Can you make launch visuals before the product is manufactured?

Yes. A product launch video before the product is manufactured is built from CAD, so no physical sample is needed to start.

What do we receive?

Stills, product films, a web 3D viewer, an AR view, and the optimized glTF and FBX files behind them. Exact deliverables are set in the brief.

How does AI fit into the work?

AI speeds up scene and variant exploration. People hold the geometry and approve every frame. AI can make a picture. It cannot make a decision.

How do we start?

Send a brief: the product, what you need to show, and where it will live. We reply with how we would approach it.

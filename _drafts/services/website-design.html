QUESTIONS

FAQ

What does a website design and development service include?

Planning, UI/UX design, development, testing and launch. Development is handled by our team or a trusted partner, and we confirm which before work starts.

How much does a custom website cost?

It depends on pages, content and functionality. Share your requirements and we will send a written scope and quote.

How long does it take to build a website?

It depends on size, content and approvals. You get dates in a written plan once the scope is agreed.

Do you create mobile-responsive websites?

Yes. Every layout is tested across phone, tablet and desktop before launch.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We keep what works, improve the rest and plan around your existing search visibility.

Do you build e-commerce websites?

E-commerce is scoped per project. Tell us about your catalogue, payments and shipping, and we will confirm the right approach.

Will my website be SEO-friendly?

Yes, we build in SEO foundations. We do not promise rankings, because they also depend on content, competition, authority and ongoing work.

Can I update my website content after launch?

That depends on how the site is built. Tell us early if your team needs to edit it regularly.

Can you integrate 3D product viewers or interactive experiences?

Yes. Our AR, VR and interactive 3D team can add 3D viewers and configurators to a website.

Do you provide website maintenance and support?

It can be added to a project. What is covered, and for how long, is agreed in the proposal.

Platform requirements

Browser-based AR works on recent iPhones and iPads through AR Quick Look in Safari, and on Android phones that support Google Play Services for AR. App-based AR uses ARKit on iOS and ARCore on Android. We confirm exact device support for every project.

FAQ

What is the difference between AR and VR?

Augmented reality (AR) adds digital objects to your real-world view, usually through a phone camera. Virtual reality (VR) replaces your surroundings with a fully virtual environment, usually seen through a headset.

What is an interactive 3D product viewer?

A 3D product viewer shows a digital model of your product on a web page. Visitors can rotate it, zoom in, inspect details and often switch colours or variants, like the demo on this page.

Can customers view furniture in their own homes?

Yes, on compatible phones and tablets. An AR experience places the furniture model in the room through the camera, at true scale, so customers can check size, fit and colour.

Can AR experiences work on mobile browsers?

Many can. Product placement works in Safari on recent iPhones and iPads, and on Android phones that support Google Play Services for AR. More advanced experiences may need an app or a specific device. We confirm this before production.

Can you create 3D models from CAD files?

Yes. We review your CAD files and references first, since format and quality decide how much preparation they need. We can also model from technical drawings and photos.

Can the same 3D model be used for AR, VR and 3D viewers?

Often, yes. One source model can feed all three, but each platform needs its own optimization and file format, for example GLB for web viewers and USDZ for Apple's AR Quick Look.

Which devices support VR experiences?

It depends on the platform you choose. Standalone headsets can run browser-based WebXR or native apps; PC-based headsets need a connected computer. We agree the target headsets at the start of the project.

How long does it take to develop an AR or VR experience?

It depends on product complexity, the number of assets, the interactions and the platform. You get a written plan with dates after we review your brief.

Can you integrate an interactive 3D viewer into an existing website?

Usually, yes. How depends on your website platform, the viewer's features and where the 3D files are hosted. We check these with your team before we start.

Do you provide both 3D modelling and experience development?

Yes. We create the 3D assets and build the interactive experience as one coordinated workflow, so nothing is lost between the model and the final experience.
